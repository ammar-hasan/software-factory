# Claims ledger

Every factual assertion spoken in this film traces to a file in the subject
repository at `/home/user/software-factory`, or to the recorded output of a
command actually run against it on 2026-09-01. Nothing here is a summary of a
summary; each source was read directly.

Two standing rules for this script, tighter than the tool requires:

1. **No result is claimed that the project has not produced.** The central
   experiment has no trials. The film says so, in the tool's own words.
2. **No comparison to any other product, tool, vendor, or model is made,**
   including by implication. The one comparative sentence in the script is a
   verbatim quotation of the project's own hypothesis about model tiers and
   harness quality, and is delivered and captioned as a quotation under test.

## Deliberately not claimed

- Any pass rate, cost, speed, or quality result. None exists: the experiment
  has recorded no trials.
- Any comparison with a named product, vendor, model, or service.
- That the three end-to-end trials say anything about model output quality.
  They do not — the model is scripted in all three, and the report says so.
