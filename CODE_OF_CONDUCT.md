# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a place where people can do good technical work and be
treated decently while doing it. That means participation is open to everyone regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Assume good faith, and extend it before asking for it.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This has a race condition" is useful; "this is
  careless" is not.
- Accept that being wrong is normal and cheap. Correcting yourself in public is a
  contribution, not a loss.
- Respect the time of whoever reads your work: say what changed and why.
- When someone tells you something you did landed badly, listen first.

## What we will not accept

Harassment of any kind, sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks,
trolling, publishing others' private information without permission, sustained disruption,
and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews —
and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers at
[ammar.hasan@disrupt.com](mailto:ammar.hasan@disrupt.com). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers will respond within 5 working days and will explain what
action, if any, they took.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban a participant. Maintainers
who do not act in good faith when enforcing this may face the same consequences.
